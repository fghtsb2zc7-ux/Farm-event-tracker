# Farm Log: getting started

The Farm Log is where we record spraying, planting, nutrients, harvests and garden work, take customer orders, and keep notes for next season.

## Put it on your phone

You'll get a link (it ends in `.ts.net`) and a login from the farm.

**iPhone**
1. Open the link in **Safari** (not Chrome or another browser).
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **Farm Log** from your home screen from now on. It opens full screen like any other app, and you stay signed in.

**Android**
1. Open the link in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

**Computer**
Open the link in any browser and sign in. In Chrome or Edge you can also click the install icon in the address bar.

## Using it

- **Log something:** tap the green **+ Log** button. Pick the type, choose from the saved list (or type a new one and save it to the list), pick the fields and crops, and tap **Save event**. Pick a future date to plan something instead.
- **Mark planned work done:** tick the circle next to it under **Next 14 days**.
- **Orders:** the **Orders** tab. Add who's coming, what they ordered, when, and what they've paid. Mark orders **paid** and **picked up**.
- **Notes for next season:** **More → Notes**.
- **History and calendar:** everything that's been logged, by the whole team.

## No signal in the field?

Keep logging. The app saves your entries on the phone and sends them when you're back in range. The bar at the top says how many are waiting.

## Problems

- **Forgot your password:** ask the farm owner to reset it.
- **Something looks wrong, or you have an idea:** tell the farm owner.
