# Morning Paper: your steps

About 15 minutes, all in a web browser. Do steps 1 to 4 before the install.

## 1. Make the paper's own Gmail
1. Create a new free Gmail account, e.g. `yourname.morningpaper@gmail.com`.
2. In that account, turn on 2-Step Verification (Google Account, Security).
3. Make an app password: go to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords in that account,
   name it "Morning Paper", and keep the 16-letter code for step 5. Don't send it in chat.

## 2. Forward your newsletters to it (in your main Gmail)
1. Settings (gear) > See all settings > Forwarding and POP/IMAP > Add a forwarding address.
   Enter the new address. Google sends a code to the new inbox: open it there and confirm.
   Leave "Disable forwarding" selected on that page; the filter below does the forwarding.
2. Create a filter: in the search bar's options, put this in **From**:
   `ft.com OR economist.com OR 404media.co OR substack.com`
   then Create filter > tick **Forward it to** (the new address) > Create filter.
   Newsletters still arrive in your main inbox as well.

## 3. Sign up for the newsletters you want in the paper
- Financial Times: in your FT account, Newsletters (e.g. FirstFT).
- The Economist: in your Economist account, Newsletters (e.g. The Economist Today).
- 404 Media: their newsletter. If you're a paid member, copy your personal full-text RSS link
  from your 404 Media account page for step 5.
- Substack: already arrives by email; nothing to do.

## 4. Apple News and Reuters
- Apple News recommendations can't be read by any outside tool (Apple offers no feed or export),
  so they can't be included.
- Reuters is included from public headlines. Reuters often blocks automatic reading, so on some
  days its story is skipped and the next source fills the spot.

## 5. During the install
When the Mac mini session opens a settings file in TextEdit, paste the new Gmail address, the
app password and (if you have one) the 404 Media link, then save and close.

## Using it
- 6:30 am: a push alert opens today's paper. Tap "Open in Books" to read the EPUB in Apple Books.
  The same paper arrives by email with the EPUB attached.
- To add a link to tomorrow's paper: email it to the paper's address with the subject `save`.
- Add the page to your home screen: in Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen.
